# Prompt Engineering: Zero-Shot and Few-Shot

## Objective
Understand the difference between zero-shot and few-shot prompting.

## 1. Zero-Shot Prompting
Zero-shot prompting means asking an AI model to complete a task without providing examples.

**Prompt:**
Classify this review as Positive or Negative: "The laptop is excellent!"

**Expected output:**
Positive

## 2. Few-Shot Prompting
Few-shot prompting means providing a few examples before asking the model to complete a task.

**Prompt:**
Review: "Amazing product!" → Positive

Review: "Very poor quality." → Negative

Review: "I love this laptop!" → ?

**Expected output:**
Positive

## 3. Key Difference
- Zero-shot: No examples provided.
- Few-shot: A few examples provided.

## 4. Learning Outcome
I learned how examples can guide an AI model toward the desired output format and pattern.

## 5. Testing Note
These are sample prompts and expected outputs for learning. They have not been tested against a live AI model yet.
